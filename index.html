03 — Process

An agent-directed pipeline.

One brief becomes a shot list, a locked character, a set of takes and a cut. You direct; the agents do the repetitive work.

01

Brief

We agree the story, the look, the length and where it will run.

02

Storyboard

Keyframes and a shot list you approve before anything moves.

03

Generate

Takes render on our own GPUs. The same character and palette hold across every shot.

04

Cut & deliver

Music, grade, captions and masters for every screen.

Built for consistency

  • Character lock across every shot, not a new face each take.
  • Re-shoot the same moment from a new camera rig without starting over.
  • Grade and cut run in the same pipeline as the generation.
  • Your footage and prompts stay on hardware you control.

One pipeline. Human approval at every gate.

04 — Studio software

The studio, in a box.

Eusyra Studio is the software behind the films: a ComfyUI-based pipeline for keyframes, video, camera re-shoots and grade. Coming soon for local and self-hosted use — your GPU, your footage, your data.

  • Runs on your own GPU, or Comfy Cloud when the local card is busy.
  • Prompt-to-shot with tools an agent can drive from the browser.
  • Character consistency, re-shoots and grade in one project.

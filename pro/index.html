01 — Setup

Three steps. About two minutes.

Each step opens in your Aionyn account or in World Studio. Sign in once with your Aitherium account and the rest follows.

  1. 01

    Confirm your pass

    Check that World Studio Pro shows as active under Billing & Packs. Invoices, payment method and cancellation are there too.

    Open Billing & Packs →
  2. 02

    Store your model key

    Add your Anthropic, OpenAI or Gemini key to your Personal Lockbox under Account → Security. It is stored encrypted and write-only, and it is never sent to or entered on Eusyra pages.

    Open Personal Lockbox →
  3. 03

    Open World Studio

    Start a project in World Studio. Your pass and your Lockbox key are picked up from your account. The Preview Content Packs arrive on December 1.

    Open World Studio → Preview Content Packs — Dec 1 →

Where your key lives

  • Eusyra pages never ask for your key. There is no form on this site for it.
  • Your key goes only into your Aionyn Personal Lockbox, encrypted and write-only.
  • Once saved, it is shown as a masked hint. Replace or delete it at any time.
  • Model usage is billed by your provider, on your own provider account.

Never paste a key into an email, a chat or a support thread.
